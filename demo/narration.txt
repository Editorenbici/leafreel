Documents are everywhere. Videos are what people actually watch.
Closed apps made video effortless. So we built an open one: PageReel.
Drop a page, or a PDF. Pick a look. Press one button.
Behind it, a pipeline runs entirely on open weights, through ComfyUI and the Comfy SDK.
A local vision model writes the script, and fact-checks it against your document.
A designed voice narrates. FLUX dot 2 draws every frame, with the character locked from scene to scene.
ACE-Step composes the music, and LTX 2.5 can turn frames into clips with ambient sound.
Then PageReel inspects its own work. A vision model looks at every frame. Garbled text, or a deformed hand? It rewrites the prompt and tries again.
Same page, completely different films: watercolor, cut paper, pixel game, claymation, chalkboard, kinetic type, and cinematic.
Edit a line, or reshoot a scene, and only what changed is regenerated.
Add your own style with one small JSON file.
PageReel. Open weights, open code, one GPU.
